Show me where to click.

GuideMe is a Chrome extension that walks you through any website, one click at a time, in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu. It doesn't do the task for you. It points at the right button and tells you why, so next time you can do it yourself.

    Why GuideMe

    Websites hide what you need. Agents hide how it's done.

    Portals bury the right link in a footer, and one wrong click sends you back to the start. GuideMe takes a different path.

    You get lost

    Hidden menus, long pages and unclear labels turn a five-minute task into an hour, especially on university and government portals.

    Agents take over

    AI agents click for you. It works once, but you learn nothing and you can't see what happened on your own account.

    GuideMe teaches

    It highlights the one right element, explains in a sentence what to do and why, and waits for you to click.

    How it works

    Four quick moves behind every highlight

    Every step goes through the same small pipeline, in a couple of seconds.

    Scan the page

    Reads visible buttons, links and inputs across the whole page, including hidden dropdown items and contact details.

    Rank what matters

    Scores items against your goal locally, with synonyms, so only the most relevant ones are sent on.

    Gemma picks the click

    Gemma 4 checks your last click and chooses the single next element, then writes a short instruction.

    You see the spotlight

    The page dims around a pulsing ring with a step badge and a clear instruction. You click, and it moves on.

    Look-ahead: while you read a step, GuideMe already reads the next page in the background, so the following instruction appears almost instantly when you click.
    Features

    Built for people, not power users

    Everything is designed so a first-time visitor can finish the task without asking anyone.

    Your language

    Ask in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu and get instructions in the same language, with right-to-left cards for Urdu.

    Spotlight highlight

    The page dims around a pulsing ring. Hover the target and the dim fades so menus stay readable.

    Dropdown aware

    Finds items inside hidden menus, opens them for you and picks the right option the moment you hover.

    Answers from the page

    Ask for a phone number, email or address and GuideMe highlights it and puts the actual answer in the card.

    Multi-page guidance

    Follows goals across several pages with a step timeline, and finishes with a clear “All done!” card.

    Resilient and fast

    Retries failed calls, switches models automatically and remembers answers, so a slow moment doesn't end your session.

    No server. No account. Your key stays yours.

    GuideMe talks straight from your browser to Google's Gemini API using your own free key. There is nothing of ours in the middle.

    • Stored only in your browser. Your API key never leaves it and is never committed anywhere.
    • Only what's needed is sent. Page title, address, your goal and short texts of buttons, links and info snippets.
    • Open source. Read every line on GitHub. Avoid pages that show information you wouldn't want sent to Google.
    Setup

    Up and running in three minutes

    Chrome only allows one-click installs from the Chrome Web Store, so you load GuideMe yourself. It's five short steps.

    1. Download and unzip

      Download the ZIP and unzip it. You'll get a folder called GuideMe-main. Keep it somewhere permanent, because Chrome loads the extension from it.

      Download GuideMe (ZIP)

    2. Open Chrome's extensions page

      Websites can't open this page for you, so copy its address and paste it into a new Chrome tab.

      chrome://extensions

    3. Turn on Developer mode and load the folder

      Switch on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, and choose the GuideMe-main folder, the one that contains a file named manifest.json.

    4. Add your free API key

      GuideMe uses Google's Gemma 4 through the Gemini API, and every person uses their own free key. Pin GuideMe from the puzzle-piece menu, right-click its icon, choose Options, paste the key and press Save.

      Get a free API key

    5. Try it

      Open any website, click the GuideMe icon, type what you want to do (for example “apply for undergraduate admission”) and press Start. Click the highlighted item and GuideMe takes you to the next step. Press Stop to finish.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is GuideMe free?

    Yes. GuideMe is open source under the Apache 2.0 license, and the Gemini API has a free tier. Each person uses their own free key from Google AI Studio.

    Which languages does it understand?

    English, Urdu script and Roman Urdu. Instructions come back in the language you asked in, including right-to-left cards for Urdu.

    Why do I have to use Developer mode?

    Chrome only lets websites install extensions from the Chrome Web Store. GuideMe is loaded directly from the folder you downloaded, which is why Chrome asks for Developer mode. Nothing is installed anywhere else.

    What is sent to Google?

    To choose the next step, GuideMe sends the page title, its address, your goal, and short texts of buttons, links and info on the page from your browser to Google's Gemini API. GuideMe has no server of its own and keeps your API key only in your browser.

    It says “Add your API key in Options” or “Cannot run on this page”

    Paste your key in Settings (the gear in the side panel) and press Save. Chrome doesn't allow extensions on chrome:// pages or the Chrome Web Store, so open a normal website instead.
